YHY AI Manager — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026

This policy describes how YHY AI Manager is intended to handle information when users connect social-media accounts and prepare or submit content.

Information processed

  • Account information returned by a platform after you authorize access, such as a platform user identifier and profile details permitted by approved scopes.
  • Content you choose to process, such as video files, captions, hashtags, and publishing settings.
  • Technical information needed to operate the service and troubleshoot errors, such as request status and platform response identifiers.

How information is used

Information is used to connect the account you authorize, prepare publishing requests, submit content when the relevant platform permission is available, show publishing status, and diagnose errors.

Tokens and account access

Access tokens, if implemented, should be stored securely and used only to provide the account connection and publishing features you request. You can revoke access through the platform's account settings. Do not publish secrets or access tokens.

Sharing

Content and account information are sent to a platform only as needed to perform the action you request or as required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as needed to provide the service, maintain necessary records, or meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards should be used. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices

You can choose what content to submit, decline to connect an account, and revoke platform authorization. For privacy requests, contact the address below.
